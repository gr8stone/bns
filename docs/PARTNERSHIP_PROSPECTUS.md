## 1. WHO WE ARE

Budget Ndio Story (BNS) is a youth-centred civic platform dedicated to making Kenya's public budgets readable, relatable, and responsive to citizens, especially young people. We sit at the intersection of public finance, civic organising, and creative media.

Since its inception, BNS has engaged **50,000+ young Kenyans** across all **47 counties** through surveys, town halls, digital storytelling, and community dialogues. When citizens understand where public money goes and are given platforms to respond, governance improves.

### Why this matters right now
- **Constitutional & Legal Right**: Kenya's Constitution (2010) and the Public Finance Management Act (2015) both enshrine the right of citizens to participate in budgeting. Yet participation remains limited, especially for the youth, aged 18–35, who make up at least a third of Kenya's population but hold little *de facto* influence over public expenditure decisions.
- **The Turning Point**: The 2024 Finance Bill protests were a turning point as they demonstrated that young Kenyans are informed, engaged, and demanding transparency. BNS exists to channel that energy into structured, constructive civic accountability at every level of government, year-round, not just at crisis points.

### Our Two-Entity Model
BNS operates through two complementary entities that reinforce each other:

## 2. THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

### For Government — MDAs & County Governments
Public participation is a constitutional obligation, but translating complex budget documents into formats that citizens can engage with remains a persistent gap. Most budget publications are inaccessible to ordinary Kenyans, particularly the youth. Ministries and county governments need credible, independent partners who can bridge the communication gap between fiscal policy and public understanding without compromising accuracy or institutional dignity.

### For Development Partners, Foundations & Private Sector
Organisations investing in Kenya's governance, health, education, finance, technology, innovation, and economic development routinely produce high-quality technical reports that never reach the communities, stakeholders, and clients they are meant to benefit. Reporting requirements are met, but public understanding and, therefore, public demand remains low. As one practitioner put it, *African organisations are doing remarkable work, but their results rarely travel beyond the impact report.*  
**BNS Studios fills that gap**: we translate institutional outputs into creative storytelling products, e.g., documentaries, podcasts, animations, and digital campaigns that real audiences actually engage with.

### For CSOs & Accountability Organisations
Peer organisations working on governance, public finance, and civic rights often lack in-house production capacity to communicate their work effectively. BNS offers a mission-aligned partner, not a commercial vendor, that understands the civic context, maintains editorial independence, and produces content that serves advocacy goals.

### For Young Kenyans
Budgets are not boring — they are the most direct expression of government priorities. BNS makes budget literacy accessible, engaging, and actionable for young Kenyans, equipping them to hold their representatives to account, participate meaningfully in national and county budget processes, and demand transparency in public spending.

## 7. HOW WE WORK
BNS is transparent by design. Our governance model ensures that commercial activity never compromises civic independence and that all partners, including government, development partners, the private sector, or civil society, can engage with confidence.

- **Non-partisan positioning**: BNS maintains strict editorial independence. We do not endorse political parties or candidates. All civic content is evidence-based and governed by our Editorial Policy.
- **Conflict of interest management**: Any transaction between BNS Foundation and BNS Studios is conducted at arm's length, documented, and approved by an independent governance adviser.
- **Transparency reporting**: BNS Foundation publishes an Annual Impact Report. BNS Studios produces independently audited financial statements, with the Foundation contribution publicly reported each year.
- **Independent oversight**: BNS Foundation's Board includes at least two independent non-executive directors beyond the founding team, providing external accountability for the mission.
- **Safeguarding**: BNS operates under a full Safeguarding Policy, with particular attention to the protection of youth participants in all programmes and community engagement activities.
- **Client eligibility**: Not every commission is accepted. All mandates are reviewed for alignment with BNS's non-partisan positioning and civic mission before engagement is confirmed.
